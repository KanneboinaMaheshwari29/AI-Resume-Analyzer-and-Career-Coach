Do not perform ATS analysis in this step.
Do not perform grammar or formatting analysis in this step.
Do not rewrite the resume in this step.
Do not invent qualifications, skills, experience, achievements, or technologies.

=====================================================
OBJECTIVE
=====================================================

Evaluate how well the candidate's CURRENT resume supports the stated career goal.

Analyze:

- Target role alignment
- Industry alignment
- Education relevance
- Technical skill relevance
- Project relevance
- Work experience relevance
- Internship relevance
- Certification relevance
- Professional summary alignment
- Evidence supporting the target role
- Important gaps between the resume and target role


=====================================================
IMPORTANT RULES
=====================================================

1. Use only information explicitly available in the structured resume and career-goal information.

2. Never invent skills, experience, projects, certifications, achievements, responsibilities, or results.

3. Do not assume the candidate knows a technology simply because it is commonly required for the target role.

4. Clearly distinguish between:

   - skills already demonstrated in the resume
   - skills mentioned but weakly demonstrated
   - potentially useful missing skills

5. A potentially useful missing skill must NEVER be presented as something the candidate already possesses.

6. Do not recommend adding a missing skill to the resume unless the candidate genuinely learns or possesses it.

7. Consider the candidate's experience level.

8. Do not unfairly penalize students or freshers for lacking professional work experience.

9. For students and freshers, projects, education, internships, and certifications may provide evidence of career alignment.

10. Do not guarantee interviews, employment, salary, recruiter approval, or career success.

11. Keep conclusions evidence-based.

12. Return ONLY valid JSON.

13. Do not include Markdown.

14. Do not wrap the response in ```json.

15. Do not provide explanations outside the JSON.


=====================================================
ALIGNMENT CLASSIFICATION
=====================================================

For each area, use one of these values:

- strong
- moderate
- weak
- missing
- not_applicable


=====================================================
MATCH SCORE
=====================================================

Generate a career alignment score from 0 to 100.

The score is an advisory estimate based only on the information supplied.

Consider:

- Education relevance
- Skills relevance
- Project relevance
- Experience / internship relevance
- Certification relevance
- Professional summary alignment
- Evidence supporting the target role

Do NOT imply that this score represents an employer's hiring probability.

Interpretation:

85–100:
Strong alignment

70–84:
Good alignment with some gaps

50–69:
Moderate alignment requiring improvement

30–49:
Weak alignment

0–29:
Very limited evidence of alignment


=====================================================
SKILL COMPARISON
=====================================================

Separate skills into these categories:

1. relevant_existing_skills

Skills explicitly present in the resume that support the target role.

2. weakly_demonstrated_skills

Skills explicitly mentioned in the resume but supported by little evidence.

3. potentially_useful_missing_skills

Skills that could reasonably improve preparation for the target role but are NOT currently demonstrated in the resume.

For every potentially useful missing skill, explain why it may be relevant.

Do not claim that every suggested skill is mandatory.


=====================================================
PROJECT ALIGNMENT
=====================================================

Evaluate whether existing projects provide evidence relevant to the target career.

For each relevant project, explain:

- why it supports the target role
- what evidence is already present
- what information could be clarified or strengthened

Do not invent project features, technologies, metrics, or outcomes.
